# IRIS National Fair 2026–27: physical display compliance

This checklist is based on the user-supplied **Abstract, Safety & Display Guidelines-NF_2026_27 (1).pdf**, sections C and the display allowed/prohibited list (pp. 4–7). It is a practical interpretation of that document, not a substitute for event staff's approval.

- **Poster:** do not print student names, personal identities, email/phone, GitHub usernames, web addresses, or QR codes encoding the URL. The guidelines explicitly prohibit student identity on the poster and allow web addresses only on the **inside of research papers or data books**. Treat a QR code pointing to GitHub conservatively as a web address, unless IRIS gives express permission otherwise.
- **Research paper/data book:** the guideline permits web addresses and personal photographs on **inside pages**, subject to normal consent and display rules. The GitHub URL can be included there, not automatically on the outside display.
- **Original evidence:** bring the original laboratory logbook and original collected data. Printed processed CSVs and a polished GitHub page are useful supplements, **not substitutes** for unmodified originals. The guideline specifically cautions against rewriting rough data neatly.
- **Physical prototype:** do not conduct experiments at the fair. Exposed moving belts, pulleys, chains, and pinch points may not be operated. Ensure electrical wiring is properly insulated and follow the event's safety checks.
- **Other prohibited objects:** awards, medals, business cards, flags, and private-company product branding are listed as prohibited for display. Do not let the historical project media or GitHub screenshots inadvertently violate your physical poster's content limits.
- **Display logistics:** a self-standing display or permitted wall display is expected; two projects share a stall. Have the research paper accessible for judge questions and design the visual narrative for judging even when students are absent.

**Publication online and display at the booth are different activities.** The repository README can identify authors online, but printing it directly as a poster without removing author identities and web addresses could violate the physical display rules.
